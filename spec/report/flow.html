The Fold Between

How a Dizzy consumer group turns commands into events: fold → claim → fold → execute → fold. Step through it, then watch what the gap between the second fold and execute lets in.

The procedure, projection and policy bodies you fill in after dizzy generate. They run only where a pill of this colour appears — a group is a deployment of a subset of them.
